Island Discovery Cruise

Half-day private cruise with lagoon stops, snorkeling, and a guide who explains marine etiquette and scenic highlights.

Features: boat, life vest, guide, light snacks, local route tips.

Duration: 4-5 hours.

Availability: Daily, weather permitting.

Sunset Heritage Walk
