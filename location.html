Destination information

Mansalay, Oriental Mindoro travel basics, routes, and practical guidance.

Use this section to explain how to get there, what to expect, and where to stay or eat while visiting the resort.

How to get there

  • Travel via Roxas or other entry points in Oriental Mindoro, then continue by van or tricycle transfer.
  • Continue by private vehicle, van transfer, or local transport to Mansalay.
  • Estimated travel time depends on your entry point and transfer route.
  • Resort transfers and local transport can be arranged upon inquiry.

Transportation options: tricycles, vans, private cars, and resort-arranged shuttles.

Nearby attractions

  • Beach activities, seaside dining, and sunset viewing areas.
  • Nearby local stops for snacks and basic supplies.
  • Scenic coastal viewpoints for photos.
  • Relaxing resort-based leisure options.

Accommodation and dining: resort rooms, nearby inns, local eateries, and seaside dining options.

Travel reminders

Bring cash, refillable bottles, reef-safe sunscreen, swimwear, and light rain protection during shoulder months.
