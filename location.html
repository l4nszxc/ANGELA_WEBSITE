Destination information

El Nido, Palawan travel basics, routes, and practical guidance.

Use this section to explain how to get there, what to expect, and where to stay or eat while traveling.

How to get there

  • Fly to Puerto Princesa or directly to El Nido airport when available.
  • Continue by van transfer, private car, or van-bus combination.
  • Travel time from Puerto Princesa is typically 5-6 hours by road.
  • Boat transfers and day-tour services are arranged locally.

Transportation options: tricycles, vans, boats, motorbikes, and hotel-arranged shuttles.

Nearby attractions

  • Lagoon viewpoints and island hopping stops.
  • Beachfront cafes and sunset decks.
  • Mangrove areas for educational eco tours.
  • Small markets for souvenirs and local snacks.

Accommodation and dining: mid-range inns, boutique resorts, seaside cafes, and seafood grills.

Travel reminders

Bring cash, refillable bottles, reef-safe sunscreen, and light rain protection during shoulder months.

Location summary

Area: El Nido, Palawan, Philippines
