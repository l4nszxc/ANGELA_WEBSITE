Travel information / tourist guide

The essentials every traveler should know before visiting.

This page covers timing, packing, safety, accessibility, budget planning, etiquette, and emergency readiness.

Best time to visit

November to May is usually the most favorable travel window for dry-season activities and smooth boat outings.

Weather considerations

Expect stronger winds or rain from June to October. Plan flexible travel dates and check marine advisories before going out.

What to bring

Light clothing, dry bag, reef-safe sunscreen, reusable bottle, cash, slippers, and a basic first aid kit.

Dress recommendations

Use breathable fabrics, swimwear with cover-ups, and comfortable footwear for beach walks and transfers.

Safety reminders

Follow guide instructions, watch tide and weather warnings, and keep valuables secure during transfers and day tours.

Local customs

Be polite, greet staff respectfully, ask before photographing people, and support local enterprises where possible.

Environmental guidelines

Do not litter, avoid touching corals, and choose reef-safe products to protect marine ecosystems.

Accessibility

Ask about accessible rooms, transport assistance, rest stops, and activity pacing before booking a package.

Estimated budget

Budget travelers can plan around PHP 3,500 to 6,500 per day excluding flights, depending on hotel and tour choices.

Emergency information

Local emergency response

Contact local authorities or your hotel desk immediately if there is an accident or severe weather event.

Travel etiquette

Arrive on time, listen to your guide, and keep noise low during early morning or wildlife-sensitive activities.

Medical readiness

Bring prescribed medicines, motion sickness tablets if needed, and basic documents for insurance or medical support.

Quick checklist

  • Book your package early for peak months.
  • Check weather and marine updates before island tours.
  • Keep a small amount of cash for transfers and local shops.
  • Use the booking form if you want a saved local inquiry.
